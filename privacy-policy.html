Privacy Policy · Last updated June 4, 2026

Data Collection

SmokOver does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All information you enter — your quit date, cigarettes per day, and pack price — is stored locally on your device only and never sent to any server or third party.

Third-Party Services

SmokOver does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or data collection services in its current version.

If advertising or analytics are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before release.

Permissions

The app may request permission to send push notifications. This is optional and used only to remind you about unlocked cards and savings milestones. No personal data is transmitted as part of notifications.

Medical Disclaimer

Values and timelines shown in SmokOver are based on published scientific literature and population averages. Individual results may vary.

SmokOver is for informational and motivational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

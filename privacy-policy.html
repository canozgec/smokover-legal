Privacy Policy · Last updated June 18, 2026

Data You Enter

The information you enter about your smoking — your quit date, cigarettes per day, and pack price — and your in-app progress are stored locally on your device only.

SmokOver does not run its own servers and does not collect your personal smoking data into any account. We cannot read what you enter.

Third-Party Services

SmokOver uses a small number of third-party services to provide ads and purchases. These services may process technical and device data under their own privacy policies:

  • AppLovin MAX — serves ads in the free version. When ads are active, it may access your device's advertising identifier and technical device data to show and measure ads. Unlocking SmokOver Pro removes ads.
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases and subscriptions. It processes a non-personal purchase identifier and your subscription status; it does not receive your name or smoking data.

These services do not receive the smoking information you enter in the app.

Permissions

The app may request permission to send push notifications. This is optional and used only to remind you about unlocked cards and savings milestones. No personal data is transmitted as part of notifications.

Medical Disclaimer

Values and timelines shown in SmokOver are based on published scientific literature and population averages. Individual results may vary.

SmokOver is for informational and motivational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. See our Terms of Use for full details.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Today, the smoking data you enter stays on your device. We are developing optional future features — such as a community or peer-support feature — that may involve some of your data leaving your device.

Before any new feature changes how your data is handled, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it (for example under GDPR/KVKK), ask for your explicit consent first. The "Last updated" date above always reflects the current version.
